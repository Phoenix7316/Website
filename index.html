ACADEMIC PROFILE / 2026

Hey, fellow observer.

Welcome to my little corner of the universe!

I'm Sharad Shukla, a graduate student in Theoretical High Energy Physics, driven by a curiosity governing the fundamental laws of our universe and the mathematical structures that underlie them.

I am also fascinated by the profound connection between mathematics and physics. I find it remarkable, how symmetry can reveal hidden patterns in nature, while abstract mathematical ideas provide a way to describe the physical world. Often, seemingly unrelated ideas come together, offering new perspectives and a deeper understanding of nature. In my view, theoretical physics is a continuous exploration of these connections. It is guided by the mathematical elegance and physical intuition, along with a lasting curiosity to understand the universe at its most fundamental level.

Beyond its mathematical elegance, theoretical physics is more than the study of what the universe is; it is an attempt to understand why it is the way it is and what it could become.

After all, the universe is vast, there is still so much we don't understand, and perhaps that is what makes the journey so fascinating.

Somewhere between an equation and a question lies a universe waiting to be understood.

And I am only one more calculation away from understanding the reality itself. Probably :)

Through this portal of mine in cosmos, I hope to share that journey of exploration, bringing together mathematical ideas, fundamental physics, and the questions that continue to deepen our understanding.
