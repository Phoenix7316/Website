## Why quantum field theory?

Quantum field theory (QFT) provides a framework for describing
quantum fields and their interactions, combining quantum mechanics
with the principles of special relativity.

Rather than treating particles as the fundamental starting point,
QFT describes them as excitations associated with underlying fields.

## The central idea

A useful way to begin studying QFT is to ask:

- What is a field, and how does it differ from a particle?
- Why do relativistic theories require a field-theoretic description?
- How do symmetries constrain physical interactions?

These questions lead naturally to the mathematical structures
used to construct and study quantum field theories.
